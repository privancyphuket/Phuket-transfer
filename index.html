Booking terms

Booking, payment & cancellation policy

Please review these before confirming your booking.

💳 Booking Fee

A booking fee is included in the total price to secure your reservation. It's non-refundable in all circumstances, including cancellation, no-show or changes of plan.

🕐 Cancellation

Cancellations made more than 24 hours before pick-up may be eligible for a partial refund, minus the booking fee. Within 24 hours, or no-shows, are non-refundable.

🌧️ Force Majeure

If a trip can't operate due to severe weather or official closure, we'll offer to reschedule your travel date. No cash refund is provided in these cases.

📅 Changes

Requests to change date or details are subject to availability. Additional cost may apply for changes in vehicle, route, date or supplier rate.
